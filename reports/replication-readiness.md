# Replication readiness

Mode B (clean-room) applies to every third-party source in this corpus: no source carries a licence that would permit Mode A high-fidelity republication inside this public repository. What IS replication-ready: the original token system, component specs, archetypes, patterns and the proposed workflow/setup specifications - all original work in `design-system/`, `design-intelligence/`, `automation-lab/` and `future-product-blueprints/`. S03 patterns may be re-implemented from the analysis; S04-S06 require vendor licences for template reuse.
